How VegaPay works

1

Load your wallet

Open VegaPay, tap Load, and choose how many of each note to add. The amount comes out of your main account balance.

2

Choose who to pay

Pick a person by username, from the QR tiles, or by their Vega ID.

3

Pick your notes

Manual: tap notes yourself. Automatic: type an amount and VegaPay finds the exact notes. No exact change? It offers to break a larger note, and only if you agree.

4

Confirm with your PIN

Create a 4-digit Vega PIN the first time, then enter it for every payment. Done, with a chime and a little celebration.

Tip: Use "Send Money (Bank)" on the Home screen for a plain account-to-account transfer. Your history labels each payment as VegaPay or Bank so you can tell them apart.
